Column sizes, columns per row, and sections

Equal and auto. A column is a number of twelfths, equal (col: an equal share of what the row has left) or auto (col-auto: as wide as its content), at each breakpoint. Every size is in the Width field of a column's settings, under Responsive; the width tools and dragging an edge turn an equal or auto column into a number, starting from the width it has.

Columns per row. A row's Columns per row field shares the line out among the columns that have no size of their own. The second section's cards are one per line on phones, two on tablets, four on desktops.

Sections. Bootstrap's containers, grouping rows. Drag rows in and out of them, choose their width in their settings, and switch views to see each one as wide as its container would be. All sizes writes one class for every size.
