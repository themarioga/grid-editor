Copy and paste

Every row, column, section, container and element has a copy tool in its drawer. Once something is copied, a paste tool shows wherever it can go: a row into a column or a section, a column into a row, a container or an element into a column. A copied row or section can also be pasted from the toolbar, with the on the right: click it, or drag it to where the copy should land.

What is copied is kept in the browser, so it can be pasted in the other editor, after a reload, or in this page open in another tab.
