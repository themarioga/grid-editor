Containers

A container holds panes, and every pane is an ordinary region: rows, columns, content areas and elements nest inside one exactly as they do at the top level. The three buttons in the toolbar add a tabs container, an accordion and a popup.

While editing, a popup is unfolded in place and an accordion opens and closes from its headers as it will on the page — what you leave open here is what the authored page opens with. Bootstrap's own JS is never asked to do any of it, so there is no backdrop and no focus trap to fight with. Double click a tab or item label to rename it.
